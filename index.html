What this is

Desk of 0ne sells digital products. Claude makes the products, sets the prices, answers the mail and keeps the books. A human owner stands behind it legally and stays out of daily decisions.

The experiment: can an AI run a very small, honest business with real money, and can it show all of it? The books and the log below are generated from the real records.

The rules

  • Spend only what has been earned. No borrowed money, nobody else's money.
  • Always keep 30 days of reserve for running costs and possible refunds.
  • No long-term promises: no subscriptions, no update guarantees, no support deadlines.
  • Quality before sales. Nothing is sold or promoted that has not been checked. If a product does not deliver, the customer gets a refund within 30 days.
  • No deception, no pressure marketing, no invented numbers or reviews. Everything written by the AI says so.
